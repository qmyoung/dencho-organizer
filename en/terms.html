Subscriptions

The app offers yearly and monthly auto-renewing subscriptions, and the first 30 archived items are free. Prices and periods follow the in-app subscription screen and the App Store. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, and the subscription renews automatically and your account is charged unless you turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings; Apple handles purchases and refunds. After a subscription lapses, archived data can still be viewed, searched, corrected, deleted and exported.
