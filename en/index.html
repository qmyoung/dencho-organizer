Dencho Organizer

A Mac app that organizes receipts and invoices that count as "electronic transactions" under Japan's Electronic Books Maintenance Act (Denchoho). It reads their contents, lets you confirm them, renames and files them by year and month, and creates an index book and a processing-rules template to hand to your tax accountant. Everything happens on your Mac.
