Summary

Dencho Organizer does not collect any data. Your receipts and recognition results never leave your Mac. The app contains no telemetry, analytics or advertising code, does not track you, and does not send your content over the network. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Data we do not collect

The provider does not receive: receipts, invoices or their contents; recognized dates, amounts, vendors or registration numbers; file names or paths; usage behavior or device identifiers; or any other personal information. The app includes no third-party SDKs and has no network permission. Recognition (including the optional Apple Intelligence) runs entirely on your Mac.

Where your data lives

Archived files, the ledger, correction memory, the To Review list and settings are all stored on your Mac: archived files, the ledger and the correction memory live in the archive folder you choose (the ledger and memory in its .receipt-mac folder), while the To Review list and settings are kept in the app's container. If you place the archive folder in iCloud Drive or another cloud service, that service syncs the files together with the ledger and memory under its own terms, and the provider of this app is not involved.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are bought through the App Store. Payment method, billing and subscription status are handled by Apple, and the provider does not receive your payment information. The app only asks the system's StoreKit whether you have an active subscription.

This website

This site consists of static pages only and uses no cookies, analytics, external fonts or scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; when you visit, GitHub may record access logs under its own privacy statement, which the provider cannot obtain.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email dencho.support@gmail.com. If the policy changes, this page will be updated.

Tax and legal information is for reference only. Please confirm with a licensed tax accountant (zeirishi).
