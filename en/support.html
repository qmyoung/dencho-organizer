Contact us

For problems or suggestions, email dencho.support@gmail.com. Please include the app version (app menu > About), your macOS version and the steps you took.

Please do not attach files containing personal information or real receipt contents; if you need to send an example, remove the sensitive parts first.

The app is an organizing tool and we do not give tax or legal advice. For reference only. Please confirm with a licensed tax accountant (zeirishi).

Frequently asked questions

System requirements

macOS 15 or later. On-device AI recognition needs macOS 26 or later and Apple Intelligence turned on in System Settings. If those are not met, the app reads by layout rules only. It remains fully usable, but check the values more carefully.

Are my receipts uploaded?

No. Receipt contents and recognition results never leave your Mac, and the app has no telemetry. See the Privacy Policy.

Which files are supported?

PDF, JPEG, PNG, HEIC, Word, Excel and OFD. A photographed paper receipt is not an electronic transaction, so keep the paper original separately. For reference only. Please confirm with a licensed tax accountant (zeirishi).

What happens when the free allowance runs out?

The first 30 items are free. After that, archiving new items needs a subscription, while archived data can still be viewed, searched, corrected, deleted and exported. See Help: Free allowance and subscription.

How do I restore purchases, manage or cancel a subscription?

Choose "Restore Purchases" in the app's Settings > Subscription, and "Manage Subscription" there while you are subscribed. Cancel in your App Store account settings. Apple handles purchases and refunds, so request a refund through Apple.

How do I keep using it after changing Macs?

Move the whole archive folder, including the hidden .receipt-mac, and choose it on the new Mac. See Help: Changing Macs and long-term retention.

The app says a file is missing or a ledger line cannot be read

Restore the missing file from your own backup. An unreadable ledger line does not stop the other records from showing. See Help: Archived records.

Does the app meet every requirement of the Electronic Books Maintenance Act?

The app helps you organize electronic-transaction data and keep a history, but whether your way of using it meets the requirements depends on your situation. For reference only. Please confirm with a licensed tax accountant (zeirishi).
